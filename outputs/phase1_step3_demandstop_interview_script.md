# Customer interview script: Agency Payment Workflow Tool

I'm looking into how small design agencies handle client payments. Could I ask you about the last time you had to manage multiple client payments?

## Questions (about past behaviour)

1. Tell me about the last time you had to collect payments from multiple clients.
2. What steps did you take to track those payments?
3. How did you keep track of which invoices were paid or overdue?
4. What tools or methods did you use to remind clients or follow up?
5. How much time did you spend on this process each week?
6. Did you ever miss a payment or have to chase a client? What happened?
7. How did you handle situations where a client paid late or partially?
8. What would make this process easier for you?
