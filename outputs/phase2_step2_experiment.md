# Phase 2 · Step 2: Demand test and its goal

**The test:** Create a simple landing page that offers the free guide and a paid add‑on. Share the link on Upwork, Freelancer, design blogs, and relevant social media groups. Ask people to sign up for the free guide and optionally purchase the add‑on.

**Why this goal:** A 10% conversion rate would show that a meaningful share of visitors are willing to pay for extra value, validating the revenue model.

## Goal set before any result existed

- What is measured: paid add‑on conversion rate
- Success means: 10%
- Time allowed: 30 days
- Fingerprint of this goal: `sha256:5a531bf07c5c4fafe94e12d0f967464a82f886471130abcb94dc1cd6320a1c05`. It was written to the decision log when the goal was set, so anyone can check the goal wasn't changed after the results came in.

Lowest goals the team may propose (config/thresholds.toml): 30 waitlist sign-ups, or 5% of visitors signing up; at most 30 days.
