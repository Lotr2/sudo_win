# Phase 2 · Step 2: Demand test and its goal

**The test:** You create a simple landing page with a sign-up form for a free preview of the guide. You share the link in freelance design communities on Reddit, Facebook groups, and design forums like Dribbble and Behance. You ask community members to sign up if they want to learn how to get paid on time.

**Why this goal:** If at least 50 designers sign up for a free preview, it shows that the problem is real and that designers are interested in a solution.

## Goal set before any result existed

- What is measured: waitlist sign-ups
- Success means: 50
- Time allowed: 14 days
- Fingerprint of this goal: `sha256:c020297c81be72fc7743974b052ca0f184dd9463bb6e040eb9220414537d9332`. It was written to the decision log when the goal was set, so anyone can check the goal wasn't changed after the results came in.

Lowest goals the team may propose (config/thresholds.toml): 30 waitlist sign-ups, or 5% of visitors signing up; at most 30 days.
