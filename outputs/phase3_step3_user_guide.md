# Rate Negotiation Guide for Freelance Designers

## What It Is
A PDF that walks you through setting, defending, and raising your design rates. It includes a framework, ready‑to‑use scripts, anchoring tactics, and email templates.

## How to Start
1. **Download** the PDF from the link provided.
2. **Open** it on your computer or tablet.
3. **Read** the first section, *Understanding Your Value*, to know how to assess your worth.

## How to Use Each Part
- **Understanding Your Value** – List your skills, review your portfolio, research market rates, and set a base rate.
- **Setting the Initial Rate** – Calculate your desired income, adjust for taxes and downtime, test with a few projects, and refine.
- **Handling Objections** – Use the scripts to respond to price pushbacks calmly and confidently.
- **Using Anchoring to Raise Rates** – Start with a premium option, explain benefits, then present the adjusted rate.
- **Cheat Sheet** – Keep this quick reference handy for on‑the‑go use during calls or emails.

## Limits
- The guide is a reference tool; it does not guarantee client approval or income.
- It is meant for freelance designers only; it does not cover agencies or other professions.
- The scripts and techniques should be adapted to your own voice and style.
