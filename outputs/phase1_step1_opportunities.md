# Stage 1: Versions of your idea and what the research found

*Your idea:* sell a digital product to other freelance designers: a kit that helps them get paid on time: proposal and invoice templates plus polite late-payment reminder emails, with a short guide on when to send what.

The team turned your idea into a few small, sellable versions, then searched the web for real people describing the problem, existing competitors, and places where these customers gather. Anything without a source is marked as a guess (ASSUMPTION).

## 1. Prompt Payment Kit (62 out of 100)

- **Who it's for:** freelance designers in their first three years
- **Their problem:** I struggle to get paid on time and don't know how to ask politely
- **What you'd make:** a pack of ready-to-use templates
- **Effort:** about 10 hours of work and $20

### What the research found

**People with the problem:**
- Freelance designers often face payment problems that can disrupt their business. ([winvesta.in](https://www.winvesta.in/blog/freelancers/5-payment-mistakes-freelance-designers-make-and-how-to-avoid-them))
**Where these customers gather:**
- Freelance designers gather on platforms like Upwork and Fiverr to find clients and work opportunities. ([fiverr.com](https://www.fiverr.com/), [upwork.com](https://www.upwork.com/hire/web-designers/))
**Existing options:**
- There is a free letter to client for making prompt payment template available ([template.net](https://www.template.net/business/letters/payment-letter-template/))

### Score

- **Evidence that people have this problem: 6 of 30 points.** Found 1 real example of people describing this problem or looking for help with it, on winvesta.in. Full marks need 5. For example: “Freelance designers often face payment problems that can disrupt their business”. Sources: [winvesta.in](https://www.winvesta.in/blog/freelancers/5-payment-mistakes-freelance-designers-make-and-how-to-avoid-them).
- **Fit with your skills, budget and time: 25 of 25 points.** You already have what it takes for graphic design, writing clear client emails and figma; it needs about 10 hours (you have about 24 a month) and about $20 (your budget is $150).
- **Places to reach these customers: 13 of 20 points.** These customers can be found on fiverr.com and upwork.com.
- **Can be built as a first version: 15 of 15 points.** It's a pack of ready-to-use templates, which the team can build, and it fits in about a month of your time.
- **Room next to existing options: 3 of 10 points.** Found 1 existing option but no complaints about them. Complaints show where you could do better.

**Verdict:** Held back because it needs at least 3 real examples of the problem (has 1).
