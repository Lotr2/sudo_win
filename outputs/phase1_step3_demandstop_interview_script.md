# Customer interview script: Polite Reminder Email Templates

I'm looking into how freelance UX designers handle client reminders. Could I ask you about the last time you sent a reminder email?

## Questions (about past behaviour)

1. Tell me about the last time you sent a reminder email to a client.
2. What was the main goal of that email?
3. How did you decide what to write in the email?
4. What did you do if the client didn't respond after the reminder?
5. Did you feel any pressure to keep the tone polite? How did you manage that?
6. How much time did you spend drafting and sending the reminder?
7. Did you use any templates or tools to help? If so, what were they?
8. What would make the reminder process easier for you?
