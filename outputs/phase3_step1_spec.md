# Phase 3 · Step 1: Product plan: Proposal & Invoice Guide for Freelance Designers

A concise guide that gives ready‑to‑use templates and step‑by‑step instructions for proposals and invoices.

**What it is:** a written guide (an e-book or PDF)

## Features

- Clear, easy‑to‑follow steps for writing proposals that win clients
- Templates for proposals and invoices that you can copy and paste
- Tips on pricing, scope, and payment terms that protect your time
- Design‑friendly layouts that look professional without extra software

## Quality checks (run automatically)

- Section **Why Proposals Matter** exists (Explain the role of a strong proposal in securing work and setting expectations.) with at least 100 words
- Section **Crafting a Winning Proposal** exists (Show how to structure content, highlight value, and personalize each pitch.) with at least 100 words
- Section **Invoice Essentials** exists (Teach the key elements every invoice must include to get paid on time.) with at least 100 words
- Section **Common Mistakes to Avoid** exists (Highlight pitfalls that can delay payments or damage relationships.) with at least 100 words
- No income promises, fake urgency or testimonials
