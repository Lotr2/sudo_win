How it works

  1. Explore the idea. The team researches the market and competitors, with sources.
  2. Test demand. A simple landing page and a sign-up goal fixed in advance.
  3. Build the product. A guide, template pack or small web tool, checked by tests.
  4. Set price and policies. Based on real competitor prices.
  5. Launch and sell. Store page and posts you approve one by one (practice mode).
  6. Learn and improve. Review results, then keep going or change course.

After each stage the team gives an honest recommendation, including "stop" when the evidence is weak. You always make the call.
