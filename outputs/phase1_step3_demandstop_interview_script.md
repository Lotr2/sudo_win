# Customer interview script: Payment Terms Cheat Sheet

Hi, I’m exploring how new freelance designers handle contract negotiations. Could I ask you about the last time you had to set payment terms with a client?

## Questions (about past behaviour)

1. Tell me about the last time you negotiated payment terms with a client.
2. What did you do to prepare before that negotiation?
3. How did you decide what terms to ask for?
4. What was the outcome of that negotiation? Did you get what you wanted?
5. If you had to do it again, what would you change?
6. What tools or resources did you use to help you write the email or contract?
7. How much time did you spend on that process?
8. Did you feel confident about the terms you set? Why or why not?
