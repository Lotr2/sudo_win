# Phase 2 · Step 2: Demand test and its goal

**The test:** Create a simple landing page that shows the template pack and a price, with a clear call to buy. Share the link on freelance marketplaces like Upwork and Fiverr, in UX design forums, and in LinkedIn groups for UX freelancers. Ask current contacts to share it with their networks, keeping the promotion free by posting in relevant communities and sending a brief email to your existing contacts.

**Why this goal:** A 10% conversion rate shows that a significant portion of the target audience is willing to pay the set price, proving the product’s value. If the rate is lower, it suggests the price or messaging needs adjustment.

## Goal set before any result existed

- What is measured: conversion rate
- Success means: 10%
- Time allowed: 30 days
- Fingerprint of this goal: `sha256:b41a4ae8f49d830db1498c41f328363617ca76bf55df36dbd3c766b0cb316797`. It was written to the decision log when the goal was set, so anyone can check the goal wasn't changed after the results came in.

Lowest goals the team may propose (config/thresholds.toml): 30 waitlist sign-ups, or 5% of visitors signing up; at most 30 days.
