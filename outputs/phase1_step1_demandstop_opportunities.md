## 4. Agency Payment Workflow Tool (66.4 out of 100)

- **Who it's for:** small design agencies
- **Their problem:** We need a system to manage multiple client payments.
- **What you'd make:** a small web tool (like a calculator or planner)
- **Effort:** about 20 hours of work and $80

### What the research found

**People with the problem:**
- Designers report clients who disappear after receiving files, dispute invoices, or drag out payment for months. ([painonsocial.com](https://painonsocial.com/blog/designer-client-problems-reddit))
- Many designers start projects without formal contracts, leading to unclear deliverables, payment disputes, and no legal protection when things go wrong. ([painonsocial.com](https://painonsocial.com/blog/designer-client-problems-reddit))
- Designers report that invoicing clients can be blocked by VAT requirements, causing payment delays. ([proz.com](https://www.proz.com/forum/business_issues/130687-vat_ue_invoice_problem-page2.html))
**Signs people want a solution:**
- Agencies and freelance teams need a system to manage multiple client payments, team access, and payment infrastructure for their whole business. ([vulta.one](https://vulta.one/en/blog/vulta-for-agencies-managing-multiple-client-payments))
**Where these customers gather:**

- The Warrior Joint Ventures forum includes conversations about ad agency invoicing. ([warriorforum.com](https://www.warriorforum.com/main-internet-marketing-discussion-forum/1378208-invoice-agency-test-assignment.html))
**Existing options:**
