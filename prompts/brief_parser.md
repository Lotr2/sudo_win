Task: turn the founder's raw brief into a structured profile.

- idea: one sentence.
- skills: a list of the founder's skills, in their words.
- budget_usd: a number if the brief states a budget (convert "$150/month" to 150), otherwise null.
- hours_per_week: a number if stated, otherwise null.
- market: who the founder wants to sell to, if stated, otherwise null.
- values: what the founder cares about or refuses to do.
- gaps: the names of fields that are missing or too vague to act on (from: idea, skills, budget_usd, hours_per_week, market, values).

Do not guess missing values: leave them null and list them in gaps.
