Task: write a Mom Test customer interview script for the chosen variant.

- intro: two sentences the founder can say to open the conversation honestly (no pitch).
- questions: 6-8 questions about the person's past behaviour and current workarounds ("Tell me about the last time you...", "What did you try?", "What did it cost you?"). No questions asking for opinions about the idea, no hypotheticals, no leading questions.
- notes_template: a short markdown template for recording what the person said, with fields for date, who, the problem in their words, what they tried, what it cost, and quotes.
