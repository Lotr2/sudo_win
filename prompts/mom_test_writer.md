Task: write a short, friendly customer interview guide for the chosen version of the idea, so a first-time founder can learn whether the problem is real without pitching.

- intro: two warm, honest sentences the founder can say to open the conversation (no pitch, no selling), e.g. "I'm looking into how people deal with X. Could I ask you about the last time it happened?"
- questions: 6-8 simple questions about the person's past behaviour and current workarounds ("Tell me about the last time you...", "What did you try?", "What did it cost you, in time or money?"). No questions asking for opinions about the idea, no hypotheticals ("would you..."), no leading questions.
- notes_template: a short markdown template for recording what the person said, with fields for date, who, the problem in their words, what they tried, what it cost, and memorable quotes. Add one line at the top with a tip: talk to 3-5 people and listen more than you talk.
